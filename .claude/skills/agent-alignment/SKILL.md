# Agent Alignment

## Purpose
Expose conflicting assumptions before they become design or code.

## Inputs
- Change Brief + mechanical Definition of Done;
- Stage 0 evidence map;
- current checkpoint;
- proposed specialist roles.

## Alignment questions
Each active role states:
1. goal and non-goals;
2. binding requirements;
3. confirmed facts vs inference;
4. responsibility/expected artifact;
5. authority limits and prohibited decisions;
6. evidence required for completion;
7. unresolved question most likely to invalidate its work.

## Output
Record:
- shared interpretation;
- disagreements;
- resolved assumptions;
- unresolved questions;
- required escalation;
- final role sequence;
- mutation owner for the next stage.

## Rules
Alignment does not force consensus. Unresolved disagreement stays explicit. Do not start Gate A while a blocking scope/evidence conflict remains hidden.
