# Agent Alignment Round

Agent alignment is a pre-execution consistency check used before substantial design/build work.

## Purpose

Different specialist roles may interpret the same requirement differently. The alignment round exposes those differences before they become artifacts or code.

## Inputs

- Change Brief;
- Stage 0 evidence map;
- repository constraints;
- known unknowns;
- proposed role sequence.

## Questions every active role must answer

1. What is the goal?
2. What is explicitly out of scope?
3. Which requirements are binding?
4. Which facts are confirmed vs inferred?
5. What artifact am I responsible for?
6. What am I prohibited from deciding?
7. What evidence will demonstrate completion?
8. What unresolved question could invalidate my work?

## Output

A compact alignment record:
- shared interpretation;
- disagreements;
- resolved assumptions;
- unresolved questions;
- required escalation;
- final role sequence.

Alignment is not consensus for its own sake. Unresolved disagreements remain visible.
